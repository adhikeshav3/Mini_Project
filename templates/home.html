Standard Operating Procedure

Grievance Redressal Lifecycle

Step-by-step procedure governing the receipt, investigation, and disposal of student grievances.

1
Lodge Grievance

The student registers their complaint online, providing specific particulars (classroom, hostel block, fee reference) and optional documentary proof.

2
Automated Routing

The system analyzes the grievance text, determines the concerned department, checks for safety emergencies, and assigns the case to an active nodal officer.

3
Investigation & Action

The assigned department inspects the issue, initiates physical or administrative remediation, and records progress notes in the official timeline.

4
Resolution & Review

Upon completion, the grievance is formally closed. The student is notified to inspect the resolution and record their satisfaction feedback rating.

Institutional Governance

Student Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC)

In pursuance of statutory university norms, the Student Grievance Redressal Committee functions as an independent, objective body to address student dissatisfaction concerning admissions, examinations, physical infrastructure, residential facilities, and campus welfare.

Key Guiding Principles:
  • Strict Confidentiality: Student identities are protected in all sensitive inquiries.
  • Time-Bound Redressal: Every case is assigned an audit log with standard SLA disposal limits.
  • Safety Prioritization: Complaints identifying electrical, chemical, or physical hazards receive immediate escalation.
Formal Appeals: dean-affairs@apex.edu

Student Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Any bona fide enrolled student of Apex Institute of Engineering & Technology (Undergraduate, Postgraduate, or Doctoral) with a valid institutional email ID can lodge grievances.

Under standard operating norms, routine academic and infrastructure issues are attended to within 3 to 5 working days. Critical safety or electrical matters are escalated immediately for same-day inspection.

When a grievance is resolved, the student provides feedback. If the resolution is deemed incomplete, the case is automatically brought before the Administrative Oversight Council (Dean of Student Affairs) for appellate review.
