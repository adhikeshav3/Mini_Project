Grievance Redressal Charter & FAQs

Standard operating procedures governing institutional grievance handling.

Any bona fide enrolled student of the institution with an active university email ID can register, submit, and track grievances concerning academics, campus facilities, hostel, examinations, and administrative services.

When a complaint is submitted, a local Scikit-Learn TF-IDF classifier predicts the category, and a Linear SVM model determines priority. Critical keywords (e.g. fire hazard, short circuit, sparks, chemical leak) trigger an immediate safety override to High Priority. Cosine similarity compares the complaint against active tickets to detect duplicates.

High-priority and emergency infrastructure concerns are addressed within 24 hours. General academic, hostel, and administrative matters are reviewed and addressed within 48 to 72 hours.

Once marked resolved, students submit a 1 to 5 star rating along with comments. In cases of low satisfaction (1 or 2 stars), administrators review the ticket history and can reassign or instruct the department to take further corrective action.
