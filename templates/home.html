Resolution Lifecycle

How The Intelligent Agent Works

From plain-text complaint submission to verified resolution, powered by scikit-learn models and rule-governed workflows.

1
Student Submits Grievance

Student enters title, detailed description, and optional photographic proof without requiring manual category or priority guessing.

2
AI Analyzes Content

TF-IDF vectorizer extracts linguistic n-grams; Logistic Regression & Linear SVM predict Category, Urgency, and Sentiment.

3
Intelligent Routing & Duplication

System scans for semantic duplicate complaints and automatically balances workload to assign an authorized staff member.

4
Staff Review & Action

Department officer inspects grievance, moves status from Assigned to In Progress, takes corrective action, and documents remarks.

5
Resolution & Student Feedback

Student monitors the live 4-stage tracking timeline and submits a 1-to-5 star quality satisfaction rating upon closure.

Institutional Transparency & Accountability

The portal provides an auditable, transparent mechanism for academic, infrastructural, and administrative redressing. Unlike unstructured email threads or manual paper applications, every grievance submitted is given a permanent immutable ID, timestamped audit log, and tracked performance SLAs.

Complete Role-Based Security Students only see their own grievances; staff view only their assigned department; administrative oversight monitors campus-wide KPIs.
